# Website Visual and Product QA

## Reference quality, not imitation

- Does the site feel editorial, spacious, and intentionally animated?
- Is the information architecture specific to a party game rather than an agency?
- Are all visuals original?
- Are there any copied phrases, exact compositions, or reference assets? Remove them.

## Home

- Hero communicates the game in 10 seconds.
- Create and Join are visible without scrolling.
- Phone-only / 4–6 / no-download proof is visible.
- Core loop is demonstrated, not merely described.
- Interactive contradiction example works with pointer, touch, keyboard.
- No fake social proof.
- First case pitch avoids spoilers.
- Final CTA works.

## Motion

- Smooth at 60fps target on representative device.
- No layout shift from animation.
- No scroll hijacking.
- Touch users can progress normally.
- Reduced motion removes continuous/parallax movement.
- Motion does not delay CTA.

## Mobile

- 320px no overflow.
- Menu fully reachable.
- Hero typography does not produce orphan letters.
- Sticky/bottom browser chrome does not hide action.
- Ticker does not create inaccessible continuous movement.

## Arabic

- RTL ordering is correct.
- Numbers/timestamps remain understandable.
- Mixed English product identifiers do not break layout.
- No artificial letter spacing.
- Punctuation and line breaks reviewed.

## Integrity

- No Lorem Ipsum.
- No fake testimonials/awards/client logos.
- No “coming soon” button that appears playable.
- No dead forms.
- Legal placeholders explicitly marked for owner.
