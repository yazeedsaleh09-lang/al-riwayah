# Google Stitch exploration brief — historical

Direct Stitch generation was unavailable in the implementation environment, and
no Stitch output was used in AL RIWAYAH. This file records the early exploration
without presenting it as final product provenance.

## Constraints that survived exploration

- Arabic-first RTL HTML text; never bake Arabic copy into imagery.
- Phone-only local multiplayer for 4–6 players.
- No SaaS card grid, purple AI gradient, stock detective, fake testimonial, or
  horror treatment.
- Motion must explain hierarchy or state change and must honor reduced motion.

## Directions considered

1. Editorial investigation dossier: archival rules, redactions, evidence stamps.
2. Cinematic legal archive: darker folders and slower, heavier transitions.
3. Experimental monochrome evidence room: brutalist type and contradiction lines.

The dossier direction was initially preferred, but implementation review replaced
it with the final void-black fracture system: one shared statement separating into
incompatible testimonies. The final decision and evidence are documented in
`design/FINAL_VISUAL_AUDIT.md`.
