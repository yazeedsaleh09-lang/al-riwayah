## North star

**A late-night social thriller around one shared story, seen through six private phones.**

The mood is not “crime scene.” It is the moment when friends realize their answers no longer line up: a room that is warm enough to feel social, dark enough to hold tension, and clear enough that the game can start in seconds.

The visual system should feel:

- social before sinister;
- clever before severe;
- cinematic without becoming a film poster;
- tactile without becoming vintage collage;
- Saudi through believable social energy and Arabic voice, not décor shorthand;
- premium through control, scale, and finish—not emptiness or giant type.

## The selected visual grammar

### 1. Shared center, private edges

The composition begins with one shared statement at the center. Four to six testimony/phone surfaces sit around it at varied angles. Each surface belongs to one player, but all initially align to the same fact.

The visual hierarchy is:

1. shared fact;
2. nearest two player surfaces;
3. remaining group;
4. one offset detail;
5. contradiction path.

This prevents “random floating cards.” Every object has a relationship to the shared center.

### 2. Alignment becomes tension

The hero and phase transitions should not continuously float. They begin in equilibrium. One detail then moves by a small, legible amount; the two conflicting answers separate; a single verdict-red relationship line appears.

Depth is shallow and tabletop-like:

- slight perspective;
- consistent light direction;
- short cast shadows;
- no impossible glass layers;
- no decorative particles;
- no orbiting cards.

On mobile, the same idea becomes a focused vertical stack: shared fact, player answer A, player answer B, contradiction link, CTA. It is not a miniature desktop table.

### 3. Darkness is a frame, not a background habit

Near-black and graphite should surround focal surfaces, not fill every section. Warm ivory paper carries readable content and relieves the page without mechanical black/ivory striping.

Suggested tonal roles:

- near-black: room, silence, pre-reveal restraint;
- graphite: secondary shells and depth;
- warm ivory: shared facts, readable explanations, legal/body content;
- muted gray: quiet metadata that remains at least routine-readable size;
- verdict coral/red: contradiction, damage, cost, decisive action;
- amber: deadline warning;
- green: connected/ready/success, always paired with icon/text/geometry.

No gradient is needed as filler. If a reflection is used, it should be a narrow physical-light cue on a phone edge or paper surface.

## Material palette

### Paper

Paper is clean, warm, and slightly fibrous. It can show pressure, fold, or edge depth, but not stains, coffee rings, burns, or scrapbook tears. Texture must disappear behind routine text before it affects contrast.

### Ink

Ink is dense and crisp. Redaction, stamps, and handwritten marks are rare and semantic:

- a redaction hides authored evidence;
- a stamp confirms a locked answer;
- a line connects a specific contradiction;
- a pressure mark can signal a revised commitment.

They are not general decoration.

### Screens

Phone/testimony surfaces show representative AL RIWAYAH UI, not fake analytics or generated interfaces. Screens are readable at their presented size. A blown-out white rectangle is not a product demonstration.

### Reflection and shadow

One consistent warm overhead source plus cool screen light is enough. Shadows should show layering and separation. Reflections must not introduce glassmorphism.

## Human presence

People are felt through:

- hands near phones;
- partial sleeves or shoulders at frame edges;
- uneven distance from the shared center;
- one player leaning in while another holds back;
- small hospitality objects only when culturally ordinary and compositionally useful.

Avoid:

- staged laughter;
- poker-table framing;
- pointing fingers;
- police interrogation poses;
- luxury-majlis showroom imagery;
- generic “Middle Eastern” patterns;
- landmarks, desert imagery, or costume as a shortcut for Saudi identity.

If culturally credible, licensed people photography is not available, the product should rely on original UI, hands-free abstract table geometry, and Arabic copy rather than force representation.

## Typography in the mood system

Typography should support the composition rather than replace it.

- Arabic body remains at least 16px in routine mobile contexts.
- Question text and timed prompts receive the largest type inside the game shell.
- Room codes and timers use tabular Latin figures where the protocol requires LTR reading.
- No negative letter-spacing on Arabic.
- No micro-labels used as atmosphere.
- No three consecutive giant headline sections.
- Display line breaks should sound natural in Saudi conversational Arabic.

The wordmark can inherit the signature idea: several aligned vertical testimony/phone edges with one displaced detail. It should not inherit editorial masthead styling from the references.

## Motion mood

Motion is restrained and causal:

- **align:** surfaces settle into a shared story;
- **separate:** one answer moves away;
- **connect:** the contradiction path appears;
- **lock:** a choice gains weight and becomes fixed;
- **inherit:** a patch creates visible new commitments;
- **resolve:** results reveal causes before totals.

Routine actions should feel immediate. The contradiction and verdict may linger briefly, but must never delay the next available action. Reduced motion replaces alignment travel with a clear static before/after composition and direct text.

## Source-cluster takeaways

### Cosmos

The useful clusters are cinematic framing, night photography, paper texture, editorial hierarchy, and organized art-direction research. Cosmos also reinforces a necessary provenance practice: discovery should retain creator/source/story context.

Because individual-save access was incomplete, these clusters set review criteria only. They do not justify copying a crop, palette, lighting setup, or image.

### Pinterest

Pinterest is reserved for broad discovery around five precise questions:

1. How do four to six friends physically occupy a table without looking staged?
2. How does a phone remain readable in low light?
3. How do paper and ink feel tactile without looking distressed?
4. What does a contemporary Saudi gathering feel like without cliché?
5. How do shallow shadows and reflections explain layered objects?

The site returned HTTP 403 in this session. Those queries remain a manual follow-up queue; they contributed no observed visual evidence and no asset.

### Fabrica and Agenciy

The transferable quality is confidence: large media, obvious navigation, complete section transitions, and visible finishing detail. The non-transferable material is the agency template itself—portfolio grids, client proof, services, awards, testimonials, metrics, and generic process narratives.

### Awwwards and creator case studies

The strongest references make one concept govern the whole site:

- Klook uses a narrative object and choice journey;
- Hyperbolic links product promise, mark, physical object, and explanatory line art;
- The ADHD Experience moves from a controlled disturbance to a pause;
- NEVERHACK varies density and section weight inside a dark identity.

For AL RIWAYAH, that governing concept is the Alibi Table. It must explain the game rather than merely decorate the page.

## Asset and provenance gate

No external image was downloaded or approved during this research.

Before any discovered visual can enter implementation:

1. resolve the original creator and originating page;
2. record the exact license and whether product/marketing use is allowed;
3. confirm that people and place depiction is culturally appropriate;
4. reject embedded third-party marks or fake UI;
5. document the asset in the project provenance log;
6. prefer recreating the principle with original CSS, SVG, Canvas, or real AL RIWAYAH UI.

## Final moodboard filter

Keep a candidate only if it answers “yes” to all of these:

- Does it feel like friends keeping one story aligned?
- Can its principle work in Arabic RTL?
- Does it remain legible at 320px?
- Does it strengthen product understanding in under five seconds?
- Can it be recreated originally without copying a source?
- Does it avoid police, forensic-enterprise, noir, cyber, and portfolio clichés?
- Does it still work with reduced motion and without sound?

Anything else is atmosphere without product value and should be removed.
