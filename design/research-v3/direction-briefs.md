# Design directions

> Historical exploration only. The owner rejected the Alibi Table, circular
> player-seat geometry, orbit, and circular mark on 2026-07-28. None of these
> directions authorizes production implementation.

These directions are deliberately different product systems, not palette swaps.
All three use the typography finding: Alexandria for restrained display moments,
IBM Plex Sans Arabic for product and long-form reading, and a mono face only for
codes and timers.

## 01 — طاولة الليل (Night Table)

- **Brand premise:** a social interrogation conducted around one shared, nocturnal
  table. The story is the centre; people and contradictions orbit it.
- **Hero composition:** compact promise and actions beside the live Alibi Table.
- **Signature visual system:** one elliptical table, player seats, shared testimony
  card, and red contradiction threads. The visual is a literal product model.
- **Typography:** Alexandria 600 for the decisive line; IBM Plex Sans Arabic for all
  interaction and evidence text.
- **Spacing/grid:** 12-column desktop split 5/7; phone collapses copy over a
  horizontally generous table. 8px base rhythm with large 32–64px structural gaps.
- **Geometry:** soft ellipse for the social arena; rectangular evidence; pill seats;
  straight threads. Each shape has a semantic role.
- **Mobile surface:** dark, immersive game shell with a warm testimony card and
  bottom thumb actions.
- **Lobby:** table seats fill in spatially, while a quiet list remains available for
  screen readers and small phones.
- **Question:** one clear question card at the centre; private evidence appears as a
  separate warm sheet, never in public room state.
- **Contradiction:** red thread connects exactly two conflicting statements; a short
  causal caption explains why.
- **Result:** the table settles into a chronological spine: cause, break, patch,
  verdict.
- **Motion:** new seats ease into their table position; answers travel inward;
  contradiction threads draw only after both endpoints exist; reduced motion uses
  instant state swaps and opacity.

## 02 — ستة مقاعد (Six Seats)

- **Brand premise:** a welcoming social circle in which six distinct voices assemble
  one account.
- **Hero composition:** large circular player map beside a friendly product promise.
- **Signature visual system:** concentric rings and six named seats; the centre holds
  the shared story.
- **Typography:** Alexandria 600, used more openly; IBM Plex Sans Arabic underneath.
- **Spacing/grid:** two balanced halves; generous negative space; modular 3-up facts.
- **Geometry:** circles dominate. A square alert intentionally interrupts the social
  harmony when answers conflict.
- **Mobile surface:** light paper surface; orbit follows copy and compresses to one
  hand-width circle.
- **Lobby:** an explicit six-seat ring; progress is legible by position and text.
- **Question:** centred prompt with answers arranged as spokes.
- **Contradiction:** a coloured interruption between two seats.
- **Result:** a circular completion dial with player contribution summaries.
- **Motion:** seats pop onto the orbit with short spring-like CSS easing; the ring
  rotates only during phase change, never during reading.

## 03 — سجل الجلسة (Call Sheet)

- **Brand premise:** an active case log assembled from testimony slips and review
  stamps.
- **Hero composition:** editorial copy beside three overlapping product documents.
- **Signature visual system:** numbered evidence slips, marginal metadata, physical
  stamps, and a persistent case rail.
- **Typography:** Alexandria 600 for titles; IBM Plex Sans Arabic for testimony and
  metadata.
- **Spacing/grid:** strict document grid with a narrow persistent rail and layered
  sheets.
- **Geometry:** rectangular papers, narrow rules, slightly rotated evidence, circular
  verdict stamps.
- **Mobile surface:** a document stack under one concise action; chronological rail
  remains visible.
- **Lobby:** sign-in ledger with status stamps.
- **Question:** a new testimony slip with answer controls at its foot.
- **Contradiction:** two slips are brought together and stamped with the discrepancy.
- **Result:** a completed dossier cover plus a chronological evidence index.
- **Motion:** slips enter as material layers, reorder along a single axis, and receive
  one brief stamp impact; no ambient floating.
