# Production direction decision

Date: 2026-07-28

## Final direction

Ship **Versioned Testimony — الرواية بنسخها** as the production hero and identity
system inside the selected light editorial direction.

The owner explicitly rejected the Alibi Table, circular player-seat geometry,
orbit, and circular mark. Those ideas are not implementation inputs. Historical
captures remain only as rejected exploration evidence.

Five independent roles reviewed the current state and three non-circular
replacement concepts:

| Concept | Reviewer result | Decision |
|---|---:|---|
| Live Testimony Editor | 65/80 and 8.3/10 | Selected after truthfulness corrections |
| Contradiction Timeline | 55/80 and 6.8/10 | Rejected as hero; chronology may inform results |
| Phone Statement Relay | 51/80 and 6.4/10 | Rejected for overlap, clipping, and generic device-collage language |

## Why Versioned Testimony wins

- It explains the real loop as one authored shared story becoming a different
  version after separate testimony, without using circles or seats.
- It can show a semantic `before → after → reason` relationship that works
  without color, pointer interaction, or motion.
- It supports deliberate desktop/mobile recomposition while keeping Create and
  Join visible.
- Its misregistered phrase is an original signature that can connect the hero,
  contradiction, patch, and causal result without forcing every route into one
  layout.
- The light paper, ink, deep green, restrained coral, and ruled revision
  surfaces suit Arabic typography and the product’s social tension.

## Truthfulness and privacy corrections

The hero is a synthetic version comparison, not a live collaborative text
editor. It never displays real private answers or suggests that players type
free-form shared text.

The production proof includes:

1. an explicit synthetic-data label;
2. one original phrase, one revised phrase, and a written reason;
3. a compact linear status strip showing activity only, never private evidence;
4. an accessible range control plus a complete static final state;
5. both Create and Join conversion paths.

## Identity

The mark is non-circular: two aligned testimony baselines, one displaced coral
revision, and one deep-green insertion caret. It contains no orbit, seat map,
round table, or Arabic letter distortion.

Alexandria 600 is limited to restrained display headings. IBM Plex Sans Arabic
remains the body and product UI family. Codes, revisions, and timestamps use a
monospace stack with LTR isolation.

## Motion

The hero performs one causal sequence: panel settles, old phrase retires, new
phrase enters, and the written explanation appears. It rests after roughly
1.4 seconds. Pointer or keyboard input compares versions; neither is required
to understand the final state. Reduced motion renders the final state
immediately.

## Asset and source boundary

No third-party visual asset entered the implementation. All signature graphics
are original semantic HTML, CSS, and SVG.

The requested Animation Pack Part 2 PDF was not present in the repository,
accessible attachments, or Documents search. Motion decisions use the owner’s
available excerpt, live reference interaction, checked-in product behavior, and
ECC guidance; no PDF-only compliance claim is made.
